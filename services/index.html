Services

Engagement Types

Flexible models designed to meet you where you are — whether you need strategic direction, hands-on delivery leadership, or structured capability building.

How I Work With Organisations

Three primary engagement models — each adaptable to your context and constraints.

🔭

Advisory & Strategic Consulting

Strategic guidance for organisations building or evolving their Enterprise Architecture function. This engagement type is suited for leadership teams that want an experienced, independent perspective on architecture strategy, tooling, governance, and organisational design.

Typical activities

  • EA maturity assessment and roadmap
  • Architecture function design and role definition
  • EAM tooling selection and business case
  • Architecture governance model design
  • Stakeholder alignment and communication strategy
  • Architecture review and challenge

Best suited for

  • CTO / CIO / Head of Architecture
  • Organisations starting or restructuring an EA function
  • Teams facing major technology strategy decisions

Engagement format

Retained advisory, fixed-scope projects, or time-limited strategic reviews.

🏗️

Embedded Architecture & Delivery Leadership

Hands-on engagement embedded within your organisation as an Enterprise Architect or delivery leader. I work alongside your teams to own architectural decisions, support delivery, and build internal capability — with a clear exit plan from the start.

Typical activities

  • Lead and facilitate architecture decision-making
  • Drive application portfolio rationalisation
  • Coach delivery teams on architecture practice
  • Manage architecture review board
  • Build and maintain EA repository
  • Align delivery roadmaps to architecture principles

Best suited for

  • Organisations undergoing significant change
  • Programmes lacking architectural oversight
  • Teams that need interim or fractional EA leadership

Engagement format

Part-time or full-time, day-rate or fixed-term contract.

🎓

Workshops & Capability Building

Structured, facilitated workshops that build practical capability in Enterprise Architecture and lean delivery. Delivered to architecture teams, IT leadership, or cross-functional groups — tailored to your current maturity and business context.

Workshop topics include

  • EAM fundamentals and practice setup
  • Business capability modelling (hands-on)
  • Outcome-driven roadmapping
  • Lean delivery for architecture teams
  • Architecture communication and stakeholder engagement
  • Application portfolio analysis

Best suited for

  • Architecture teams building shared practices
  • IT leadership seeking structured learning
  • Organisations onboarding new EAM tooling

Engagement format

Half-day to multi-day workshops, remote or on-site.

How I Work

What you can expect from every engagement.

Context First

I invest time upfront to understand your specific business context, constraints, and goals before proposing any approach or solution.

Transparent & Direct

I share my honest assessment, including where I disagree, and communicate clearly about risks, tradeoffs, and limitations.

Outcome Focused

Every engagement is scoped against specific outcomes, and we review progress against those outcomes regularly — not just delivery milestones.

Capability Transfer

My goal is always to leave your organisation stronger than when I arrived. I build internal capability alongside delivering results.
