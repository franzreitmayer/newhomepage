About Franz Reitmayer

Enterprise Architect, lean practitioner, and business-outcome-driven delivery leader.

Profile

I am an Enterprise Architect and delivery practitioner with a focus on helping organisations bring their technology landscapes into alignment with strategic business goals. My work sits at the intersection of architecture, governance, and lean delivery — where decisions about systems and structures directly shape what an organisation can and cannot do.

I am a strong believer that architecture should serve the business, not the other way around. That means keeping models pragmatic, governance lightweight but effective, and every architectural decision traceable to a business outcome.

Whether working as an embedded architect, an advisor, or a facilitator, my goal is always the same: to help organisations build the clarity and coherence they need to deliver value with confidence.
